What is the MEPES MP3500?

A ruggedized portable energy package that delivers 3,500 watts of energy with 7.6 kilowatts of internal energy storage. That is enough power to operate a modern full-sized 27.9 cu ft refrigerator for almost 4 days — almost indefinitely with optional portable solar panels and a backup generator.

Quick and simple to operate: just turn on the two sets of switches and press start. Packing up is just as easy — unplug the charging cord, turn off the switches, close the case, slide the MP3500 in the back of a truck or trailer, and go. Once at your destination, open the case, plug in the optional portable solar panels, and you are ready to power your equipment.

General specifications

Case

Waterproof Nanuk Polypropylene Hard Case. External dimensions: 34" x 29" x 17.75". Coasters, lifting handles, and retractable handle for rapid deployment.

Batteries

Two US-made SimpliPhi/Briggs & Stratton Phi 3.8 LiFePO4 batteries. 7.6 kWh total storage. Safest lithium chemistry — no cobalt, no thermal runaway risk.

AC output

Two built-in 20 Amp AC circuits with 20 Amp industrial grade receptacles.

Charging

30 Amp twist lock charging port with power cord and 20 Amp adapter. Capable of serving loads while charging. Generator and grid compatible.

Solar input

Port for permanent or portable solar panels. Optional 800W foldable suitcase panels available — up to 3,600W total solar capacity across 3 strings.

Safety

Will not backfeed to the grid. Protects utility workers from accidental shock. Not intended for permanent electrical system connection. Patent pending.

Inverter specifications

3,500W pure sine wave inverter output with 7,000W surge. 120V AC at 60 Hz for North American use.

MPPT technology

State-of-the-art solar MPPT with up to 99% efficiency. Charging preset to PV priority — solar charges batteries when available, generator or grid takes over when not.

Smart load management

Load output preset to PV priority. Solar powers loads and charges batteries. Utility or generator covers loads and charging when battery levels are low.

Display & protection

Dynamic LCD and intelligent LEDs for system info and error alerts. Complete hardware protection including short circuit, over-voltage, and overload safeguards.

Energy storage specifications

Lithium Ferro Phosphate (LFP) — the safest lithium-ion chemistry available. Maintenance-free with no ventilation or cooling required.

Capacity

75 Ah per battery. 3.8 kWh DC per battery at 100% depth of discharge. 48–56 VDC operating range.

Performance

98% operating efficiency. Max continuous discharge: 37.5A DC (1.92 kW). Max 10-minute discharge: 60A DC (3.07 kW).

Durability

10,000+ cycles at 80% depth of discharge. Operating temp: -4 to 140 °F. Less than 1% self-discharge per month. No memory effect.

Certifications

Certified to UL 1973. UL, CE, UN/DOT and RoHS compliant components. Designed and manufactured in California, USA.

Solar input specifications

PV input

Max PV voltage: 145 VDC. MPPT range: 60–115 VDC. Max input power: 4,400W DC. Max input current: 80A.

Optional portable panels

800W lightweight foldable suitcase panels. Open circuit voltage: 94.4V paired. Folded dimensions: 27.9" x 33.7" x 3.2" per section. Weight: 30.2 lbs per section.

Expandable solar

Panels come in 400W sections (2 per unit). The MP3500 supports 5 strings with individual breakers for up to 4,000W DC total solar capacity.
