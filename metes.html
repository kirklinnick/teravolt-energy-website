What is a METES?

METES is a Modular Expandable Transportable Energy System manufactured in a controlled environment in a fully self-contained unit (or set of units) that can be ordered to your requirements, built, delivered, then set up in a minimum amount of time.

Transportable

Designed to be shipped safely by truck, train, or ship to any location served by normal shipping methods.

Expandable

Individual modules can be matched with different METES models to meet any energy need.

Built to last

Highest quality systems with name brand equipment, designed for long-term performance.

What METES Models are available?

The METES Hybrid

Self-contained system starting with a Master unit housing 8kW/DC of solar power, American-manufactured Lithium Ion (non-explosive, non-corrosive) batteries, and generator-ready design.

The METES Solar Master

Grid-capable solar-only METES with final hookups, supplying 8kW/DC of solar power and all equipment needed to connect to end loads and/or the grid.

The METES Solar Slave

Solar-only modules that add up to an additional 24kW/DC of solar capacity to either the Hybrid or the Solar Master.

METES Central Commercial Systems

Larger-scale systems with a Central Master unit, multiple solar units, and options for battery-only and generator-only modules.

What optional equipment is available?

A basic system starts with 8kW/DC of solar capacity standard on all module units. The METES Hybrid includes 7.2 kW of battery storage with options to add battery capacity in 3.8 kW increments and comes generator-ready. Optional equipment includes atmospheric water generation, electric vehicle charging stations, space for living or working build-outs, and other customized features.

Generator options

Multiple sizes and fuel types available (ordered alongside the METES system).

Trailer-ready

Standard models are ground-based and can be delivered pre-mounted on gooseneck trailers.

Custom add-ons

EV charging, water generation, and bespoke site features.
