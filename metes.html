What is the METES™?

The METES™ is a large-scale modular energy system housed in a standard shipping container. It is designed for both off-grid and grid-tied applications where significant power capacity is needed. The system is expandable, transportable, and engineered for rapid deployment.

Options include added energy storage, larger generators, electric vehicle charging, up to 32 kW/DC of solar capacity (8 kW/DC per added slave unit), atmospheric water generation, and a number of custom features.
