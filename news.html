Newest update: MP3500

Since the founding of its predecessor company in 2002, TeraVolt Energy has gone through several transitions: as a commercial electrical contracting company, as a solar installation company, and as a developer of commercial solar and energy storage projects. With the COVID‑19 shutdown, the company moved into another direction. Demand for fixed installations diminished with changes in federal and local incentives, but niche markets still require solar and energy storage.

One of these niche markets is the manufacture and sale of prefabricated energy packages that can be easily moved wherever electricity is needed and set up with no specialized training. TeraVolt Energy has prototyped a fully self‑contained portable energy package called the MP3500. The unit is housed in a hard poly case, is comprised of 7.6 kWh of Lithium Ion battery storage, and can be charged from either a generator or from the grid. It is heavy—around 200 pounds—and intended for disaster response, military operations, Customs and Border Patrol use, and longer‑term remote needs.
